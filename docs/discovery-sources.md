# Discovery sources

## Official connectors

Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby are first-class structured connectors. SmartRecruiters follows after
live contract verification. Workday, iCIMS, Jobvite, and custom employer pages use conservative
per-source adapters only where public access and applicable policies permit it.

## Broad sensors

Brave Search and Adzuna discover potential listings. Their snippets, age, and classification are
not authoritative. A result enters processing only after the official listing is located and
verified.

## Prohibited behavior

FirstSeen does not scrape LinkedIn or Indeed, bypass CAPTCHAs, automate authenticated career-site
sessions, evade anti-bot controls, or use private/hidden APIs without authorization. Unsupported
sources are reported and handled manually.
