# Product requirements

## Goal

Reduce the time between a suitable software-engineering job appearing on an official employer
site and Ilan seeing and applying to it.

## Candidate constraints

- Single candidate with eight months of full-time software-engineering experience.
- Full-time jobs only.
- Zero to one required year is ideal; no more than two required years is eligible.
- Any clearance requirement, ability to obtain a clearance, Public Trust, or equivalent suitability
  requirement is excluded. An ordinary background check is not a clearance.
- Eligible onsite/hybrid areas are South Florida within the configured Miami region, the configured
  DMV region around Fairfax, Virginia, and New York City's five boroughs. New Jersey is excluded.
- Fully remote work is eligible when the employer can employ the candidate in an accepted
  jurisdiction.
- The candidate needs no US sponsorship. Any salary and industry are acceptable.
- Role priority: general SWE, full-stack, backend, frontend, AI engineering, embedded/C, internal
  automation, and customer-facing engineering.

## Product scope

- Monitor manual, priority, and referral companies approximately hourly.
- Run broad discovery every four hours initially.
- Verify every discovered job through an official employer or ATS source.
- Detect new, changed, duplicated, stale, closed, and reopened jobs.
- Maintain Hot/New, Strong, Possible, Review, Referral, Saved, Applied, and Dismissed views.
- Explain eligibility, strengths, weaknesses, freshness, location, source confidence, and urgency.
- Send exceptional-match alerts and an 8:00 AM Eastern Slack digest.
- Open official applications for manual completion with Simplify.

## Explicit exclusions

No full auto-application, Gmail access, automated referral outreach, LinkedIn or Indeed scraping,
CAPTCHA circumvention, or unnecessarily complex applicant tracking.
