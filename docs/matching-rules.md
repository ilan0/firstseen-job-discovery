# Matching rules

Matching is versioned and explainable. Deterministic rules own employment type, maximum required
experience, clearance/Public Trust exclusions, sponsorship compatibility, geographic eligibility,
deduplication, lifecycle, queue routing, and notification eligibility.

AI may extract facts and generate concise explanations. It cannot silently change hard decisions.
Missing or contradictory evidence produces `review`, not an unjustified exclusion or recommendation.

Recommendations expose separate dimensions for skills, required experience, role family,
freshness, referral advantage, location, competition proxy, extraction confidence, source
confidence, and urgency. There is no opaque master match score.
